# 🇳🇬 NYCN Ireland

**National Youth Council of Nigeria, Ireland Chapter**  
*Empowering Nigerian Youth in Ireland* | Registered NGO (RN:794640)

## 🌟 Who We Are

NYCN Ireland is the official chapter of the National Youth Council of Nigeria serving Nigerian youth living in Ireland. We are a community-driven organization dedicated to empowering young Nigerians through personal development, academic excellence, professional growth, and cultural connection.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To create a vibrant community where Nigerian youth in Ireland can:
- **Connect** with like-minded individuals
- **Grow** personally and professionally
- **Thrive** in their academic and career pursuits
- **Preserve** and celebrate Nigerian culture
- **Access** opportunities and resources
